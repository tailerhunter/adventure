# Nibbler's Bunny Bakery
by: Tailer Hunter
theme: forest

## bakery

![Nibbler baking in the bakery](images/bakingbunny.png)

You are Nibbler, a little bunny who has just inherited his grandmother's bakery.

The bakery has been closed for months, but tomorrow is finally opening day! You want everything to be perfect, but the shelves are empty and the kitchen needs some work.

You'll need to gather ingredients, find Grandma's old recipes, and decorate the bakery before tomorrow.

Your strawberry cake is a huge success!

Everyone loves Grandma's recipe, and Nibbler knows she would be proud.

The bakery is filled with happy customers, and your first day is better than you could have imagined!

THE END
